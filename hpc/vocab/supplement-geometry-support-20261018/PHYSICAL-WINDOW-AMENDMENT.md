# Physical-window amendment before outcomes

Author: James Edward Ball. Declared 18 October 2026 before registered development or confirmation simulations. This amendment supersedes the class-cell feature generator in `PROTOCOL.md` and the aggregate-model limitation in the preflight clarification. The earlier declarations remain preserved. No outcomes were produced with that aggregate generator beyond small artificial unit-test fixtures.

## One vector per window

The user requires an interval procedure applicable to multi-label image features. Each synthetic physical window now has one complete baseline vector, one semantic-route vector and one appearance vector. Every class labelling that window reuses those exact vectors. Only metadata and latent random variables enter this generator; no observed representation or language-alignment effect is used.

Let J_w be the window's admitted-label set. Its baseline conditional mean is the average of A_j + 0.7 V_j over j in J_w, plus 0.6 L_g for its geographic bin. The semantic mean adds beta times the average of S_j. Appearance mean averages A_j and adds 0.3 L_g. Empty admitted-label windows contribute no scored class mean or class geographic count; they remain in the sampling design and split-normalisation denominator.

Each window's image noise is the sum of a shared cell vector (coefficient 0.7), the mean of shared cell-family vectors over the window's distinct represented families (0.5), an independent route/cell-label-bundle vector (0.5), and an independent route/window vector (1.0). A bundle is the exact sorted admitted-label set within the sampled cell; multiple windows with that same bundle share its route-specific bundle innovation. Cell and cell-family innovations are shared between the two image routes. Every complete window vector, including all noise terms, is reused across its labels. Independent route/window innovations are averaged when constructing class-cell means and therefore retain the covariance due to shared windows.

Appearance noise uses independent cell, mean cell-family and window innovations with coefficients 0.4, 0.3 and 0.7. Region effects retain the declared five- or twenty-degree construction and coefficients, and are shared across all windows in that region. The low-noise multiplier remains 0.5. In the heterogeneous regime the multiplier is one scalar per window: sqrt(1 + mean_{j in J_w}(22/s_j)), where s_j is fixed retained test-cell support. This scalar multiplies its cell, family, bundle and window innovations; region effects retain their separately declared coefficients. The same scalar is reused across all labels of that window. No target-class-specific feature or noise is added after window generation.

## Exact analytic truth

Define the fixed K-by-K mixture matrix M. For target class c, average equally over retained test cells containing c; within each such cell average equally over its windows labelled c; within each selected window give each j in J_w weight 1/|J_w|. Thus M_cj is the expected contribution of latent class j to the cell-balanced prototype for target c. Every row of M sums to one. The matrix is computed solely from frozen retained window labels and class-cell counts.

Let ell_c be the equally weighted mean geographic location vector over retained test cells containing c. Exact population means are M(A + 0.7V) + 0.6 ell for the baseline, that mean plus beta MS for the semantic route, and MA + 0.3 ell for appearance. The fixed text prototype remains normalise(A_c + 0.6 ell_c + 0.8 S_c), or the declared independent text in its diagnostic. The population geographic histogram remains the exact retained class-window count histogram across all splits. The population RSA targets therefore remain analytic and independent of each repetition's noise and bootstrap draws. At beta zero, paired population differences remain exactly zero.

Fresh-cell sampling, conditional-on-retention scope, admission diagnostics, candidate interval formulas, spatial weights and all acceptance bands are unchanged. The fixed development and confirmation seed namespaces remain unchanged because no registered outcomes have been generated. Confirmation retains the preflight counts of 5,000 for every beta-zero scenario and 1,000 for each beta-0.4 scenario. Tests must verify that reconstructed prototypes equal direct aggregation of complete physical-window vectors, M matches brute-force window/cell averaging, and shared-window innovations are identical wherever the same window appears under multiple labels.

This construction addresses complete multi-label feature reuse. The dimension-24 Gaussian and finite-template population limitations remain. Empirical admission still requires independent confirmation and a separate applicability assessment using outcome-blind retained-training feature diagnostics. Fourfold sampling remains a global support-budget diagnostic; targeted rare-category acquisition would need its own declared selection and resource plan.
